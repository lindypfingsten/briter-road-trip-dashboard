# BRITER™ Road Trip Dashboard

**Manage Processes. Lead People. Move Organizations Forward.**

The BRITER™ Road Trip Dashboard helps leaders connect mission and strategy to the evidence they review with their teams. It grew out of a leadership exercise that used a road trip to explain measurement: the route sets the direction, and the dashboard tells you what deserves attention along the way.

[Open the interactive BRITER™ Dashboard Builder](https://lindypfingsten.org/dashboard-builder.html)

## How I use it

I start with the mission. What should people experience because of the work we do? Then I choose measures that help the team see whether we're delivering on that promise and what might be getting in the way.

If the goal is for new members to feel like they belong, I'd look at 90-day retention alongside what members say about feeling welcome. I'd also ask what staff are seeing. Are new members getting a clear next step? Is the welcome desk covered when they're most likely to need help?

From there, I use the dashboard to agree on one action, who owns it, and when we'll check back. If we adjust welcome desk coverage, we can look at whether more new members get oriented and whether they tell us they feel more connected. That's how I bring the mission into a day-to-day operating decision.

## Try it with a fictional scenario

Imagine a community center wants new members to feel welcome and stay engaged through their first 90 days. Enter that strategy in the builder. Then use the nine lenses below to choose what you would watch. For each lens, enter a goal, current state, target, evidence source, and owner. The values below are illustrative.

| Lens | Example to try |
| --- | --- |
| **OKR — Objectives and Key Results** | Improve first-90-day engagement. Increase the share of new members who complete an orientation from 40% to 65%. |
| **KPI — Key Performance Indicator** | Track 90-day retention for each new-member cohort. Use 72% as the baseline and 80% as the target. |
| **KPA — Key Performance Alignment** | Clarify who owns follow-up when a new member misses orientation and give that person time to do it. |
| **KMI — Key Mission Indicator** | Ask new members whether they feel a sense of belonging. Review their ratings alongside optional comments. |
| **KCI — Key Condition Indicator** | Track how often peak-hour staffing leaves the welcome desk without coverage. |
| **KBI — Key Behavior Indicator** | Observe whether staff offer each new member an orientation and explain the next step. |
| **CSF — Critical Success Factor** | Confirm that orientation slots are available during evenings and weekends. |
| **KMPE — Motivation & Psychological Energy** | Ask staff which parts of onboarding energize them and which make the work harder to sustain. |
| **KTI — Key Trust Indicator** | Ask whether staff feel safe raising onboarding problems and track whether leaders close the loop on reported issues. |

Try starting with the KPI and KCI. If retention falls while the welcome desk is frequently uncovered, compare those patterns and ask staff and members what they experienced before deciding what to change.
